## One number problem, solved

Your team calls and texts customers from personal cells, a clunky phone system, or not at all — and nobody can see who said what. BTI Voice puts your company numbers in one shared app: every call, text, and voicemail in one place, visible to the whole team.

## What your team gets

- **Shared texting inbox** — every agent sees customer conversations; group MMS, scheduled sends, templates, after-hours auto-replies
- **Business calling from any computer** — no desk phones; each employee gets their own number with your company caller ID, verified caller attestation (fewer "Spam Likely" flags)
- **Voicemail with transcripts** — read voicemails; recordings and AI summaries attached to the customer automatically
- **AI call notes** — calls recorded (with automatic disclosure), transcribed, and summarized so nothing gets lost
- **Phone tree / routing** — press-1 menus, direct lines per employee, missed-call auto-text
- **Compliance built in** — carrier-registered business texting (A2P 10DLC), STOP/opt-out handling, and a consent audit log — the stuff that gets unregistered senders blocked in 2026
- Works in any browser. No hardware, no installation.
